## Ready-to-paste article copy

### Should you freeze bread whole or sliced?

Decide what you want to do when you open the freezer. For weekday toast or school lunches, slice the cooled loaf and pack it in the portions you usually pull out. If you're saving bread to serve with soup on Sunday, freeze a half loaf or a whole one. A half loaf gives you a fresh cut face at the table and leaves the other half for another meal.

| What you'll eat | What to freeze | How to use it later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A slice of toast | Single slices with parchment between them | Put a slice in the toaster from frozen |
| Sandwiches for two | A stack of four slices with parchment between pairs | Thaw the pairs you need on the counter |
| Bread for the table | A half loaf or whole loaf, wrapped snugly | Thaw at room temperature in its wrapping, then refresh a crusty loaf in the oven if you like |

Picture one loaf destined for two school lunches and Saturday breakfast. Tuck four slices into a freezer bag for the lunches, with a scrap of parchment between pairs. Wrap the remaining half separately. When someone asks for a sandwich on Thursday morning, the other half can stay put.

### What kind of bag prevents freezer burn?

Check the box for **freezer use** and check the fit against your widest slice. The bag should close without bending the bread or leaving the zipper stretched. Use parchment between portions so they lift apart, then put the whole stack inside the freezer bag. Press out excess air before sealing. Label the bag with the type of bread and the freeze date.

If you like to freeze a whole round sourdough loaf, check that the opening can clear the loaf at its widest point. A freezer-safe container with a tight lid also works. For a loaf that fits loosely in a container, wrap the loaf snugly first to limit the air around its crust. The [National Center for Home Food Preservation](https://nchfp.uga.edu/faqs/packaging-materials/category/faq-freezing) recommends freezer-weight bags over ordinary bread wrappers for freezing.

### How to thaw bread without thawing the entire bag

For toast, take out the slices you want and seal the bag before you put it back. For a soft sandwich, thaw a portion on the counter until it bends easily. Leave a larger loaf wrapped while it thaws at room temperature. Once thawed, remove the wrapping and briefly warm a crusty loaf at 350°F if you want a crisper shell. [King Arthur Baking's bread-freezing guide](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2023/12/20/freeze-bread) gives timing and texture guidance for different shapes. [Illinois Extension](https://extension.illinois.edu/food-safety/freezer-storage) lists three months at 0°F as a best-quality storage window for bread packed in a heavy-duty freezer bag.
