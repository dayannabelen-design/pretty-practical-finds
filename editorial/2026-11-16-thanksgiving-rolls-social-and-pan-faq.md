### Should you use glass or metal for make-ahead dinner rolls?

Use the pan specified in your recipe. King Arthur's soft dinner rolls call for a metal 9 × 13-inch pan or two 9-inch round cake pans. Its brown-and-serve recipe calls for finishing the frozen rolls on a parchment-lined baking sheet. If you use glass bakeware for a recipe that calls for it, follow its manufacturer's temperature instructions. Pyrex advises avoiding sudden temperature changes and using a fully preheated oven. For rolls that go straight from freezer to oven, transfer them to the baking sheet called for by the recipe.

Sources: [Soft Dinner Rolls](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/soft-dinner-rolls-recipe), [Homemade Brown and Serve Rolls](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/homemade-brown-and-serve-rolls-recipe), [Pyrex use instructions](https://pyrexhome.com/pages/product-warranties-safety-and-usage).
