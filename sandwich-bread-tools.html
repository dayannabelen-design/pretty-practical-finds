Baking tools

The tools I’d start with for homemade sandwich bread

A short equipment list for baking sandwich bread in a 9×5 loaf pan, with everyday substitutes and clear buying priorities.

Start with a 9×5-inch loaf pan

Check the recipe’s pan size first. A dough sized for a 9×5-inch pan will sit higher in a smaller pan, so the loaf shape and baking time may change. If you already have a suitable pan, use it for your first few loaves and see whether it releases easily.

Weigh the flour when the recipe gives grams

Put the mixing bowl on a digital scale, press tare, and add flour until you reach the recipe’s weight. A scale is most helpful when you bake the same loaf more than once and want to know whether a change came from the recipe or from how you scooped the flour.

Mix in a bowl you already have

A roomy bowl and a sturdy spoon get the dough started. If the recipe calls for kneading, finish by hand on the counter or use a stand mixer you already own. A bowl scraper is handy for sticky dough if you bake often.

Cool before slicing

Take the loaf out of the pan according to the recipe and let it cool on a rack so air can circulate underneath. Use a long serrated knife and gentle strokes. Warm bread is tempting, but giving the loaf time to cool helps keep soft sandwich slices from squashing.
