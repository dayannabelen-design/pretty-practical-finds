How to use a digital kitchen scale for bread

Learn how to tare a scale, weigh flour in grams, and choose a scale that fits your bread bowl.

Set the scale to grams

Place the scale on a firm, level counter. Set your empty bowl on top, switch the unit to grams, and press tare. The display should read zero with the bowl still on it. Add the first ingredient until the scale reads the amount in the recipe.

Weigh small ingredients separately

Weigh salt and yeast into a small cup first. It is easier to remove an extra pinch from a cup than from a bowl of flour. If a basic scale struggles to register a tiny amount and the recipe provides a teaspoon measure, use that measure.

Check bowl fit and capacity before shopping

Put your biggest mixing bowl on your current scale. Can you still see the display? If you shop for an upgrade, look for a readable screen, a clearly marked tare button, and enough capacity for the bowl and the full batch together.
