### Will my mixing bowl cover the Escali Primo's display?

It depends on the bowl. A narrow-footed bowl with a flared rim can sit securely on the platform while still blocking your view from above. If the bowl is beside you while you shop, set it on the counter and look at how far the rim extends beyond the base. Compare that overhang with a top-down photo of the scale. The Primo's listed 8.5 × 6-inch footprint describes the entire scale, so it cannot tell you by itself whether the display will stay visible. [Escali specifications](https://kitchensupply.com/products/primo-digital-scale)

If you already have the scale, try your usual bowl empty before pouring. Center it on the platform and check that you can read the digits and press tare from your normal standing position. If it blocks the display, weigh ingredients in a smaller bowl and transfer them. For frequent big-bowl batches, compare a model with a screen that pulls forward, such as OXO's 11-lb food scale. A pull-out screen solves visibility. You still need to count the bowl in the total load. [OXO product page](https://www.oxo.com/11-lb-stainless-steel-food-scale-w-pull-out-display.html)

If you're weighing water and flour for a small starter feeding, a jar or narrow bowl may be all you need. See [the sourdough starter basics guide](https://prettypractical.netlify.app/sourdough-starter-basics) for feeding ratios.
