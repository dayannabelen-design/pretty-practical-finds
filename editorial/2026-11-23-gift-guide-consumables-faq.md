## Ready-to-paste addition

A refill of something they use every week can also make a thoughtful gift. Precut parchment is useful when it matches their pans: half-sheet liners for a cookie baker, or rounds sized for their cake tins. Check the dimensions on the package before buying. A favorite vanilla or flour is another possibility if you know the exact product they use. If you're unsure, ask for one pantry staple they'd like replenished.
