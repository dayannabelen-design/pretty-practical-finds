8×4 vs. 9×5 loaf pans for sandwich bread

How loaf-pan size changes the shape of homemade bread and which pan to buy first.

Match the pan to the recipe

Start with the dimensions printed in the recipe. If your favorite sandwich bread was developed for a 9×5-inch pan, that size is the simplest first purchase. If most of your recipes specify a smaller pan, start there.

Expect a shape difference when you swap

With the same dough, a wider pan spreads the dough across a larger base. A narrower pan gives the dough less room to spread. The height and look of the finished loaf can change even when you use the same ingredient weights.

Measure what you own before ordering

Measure across the inside of the pan near the rim and check the depth. Product labels often round the dimensions, and two pans with similar labels can differ in depth or taper. If you swap pans, watch the dough as it rises and use your recipe’s doneness cues as you bake.
