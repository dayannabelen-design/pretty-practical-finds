## Fresh buyer research

A [banneton-size discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Sourdough/comments/1ryzqbu/bowl_size_makes_a_difference/) asks about choosing a basket for a recipe using 500 g flour. [King Arthur's proofing-basket guide](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2023/01/25/bannetons-brotforms-proofing-baskets) describes basket use. Its round and oval product listings specify dough capacity, which is more useful than relying on diameter alone.

Possible later gift-guide detail: check the recipient's usual total dough weight and loaf shape against the basket's stated capacity. Flour weight alone leaves out water, starter and salt. This research does not establish keyword volume, product testing or a new paid recommendation.
