### Ready-to-paste copy

**Can the Escali Primo weigh 1 gram of yeast?** Its display moves in 1 g steps. In response to a shopper who asked about the lowest usable weight, an [Escali product expert](https://www.homedepot.com/p/questions/Escali-Primo-White-Digital-Food-Scale-P115W/203879736/3) advised using a different scale for amounts from 1 to 5 g. If a long-rise pizza dough calls for 2.5 g of yeast, use a precision scale whose stated working range covers that dose. A recipe-provided spoon measure is another option.

**Can it weigh water for bread?** Yes. Put your mixing bowl on the scale, choose grams, and tare the bowl before you pour. Stop at the water weight in your recipe. The same two-button routine works for flour or a starter feeding. The [manufacturer lists a 5,000 g capacity and a four-minute inactivity shutoff](https://kitchensupply.com/products/primo-digital-scale). Weigh the bowl and full batch together when checking capacity.

Try the gram-by-gram routine the next time you [feed your sourdough starter](https://prettypractical.netlify.app/sourdough-starter-basics).
